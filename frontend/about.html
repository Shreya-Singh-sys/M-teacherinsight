About TIE Hacks
A Coach, Not a Monitor

Teacher Insight Engine is an AI Co-Pilot for educators, providing 100% private, objective, and data-driven insights for self-growth.

Our Mission

We believe every teacher deserves the opportunity to grow without the fear of surveillance. TIE is designed to be a personal coach, helping educators understand their strengths through multimodal AI analysis.

Ethical AI First

TIE is fundamentally designed as an ethical AI tool. It is NOT a surveillance system. Teachers choose when to upload recordings after class.

100% Private

Only you can see your reports. All data is encrypted, and no raw video is stored long-term, only anonymized embeddings.

Objective & Fair

Our AI ensures fairness across accents, teaching styles, and backgrounds. Every teacher gets unbiased, actionable feedback.

Growth Focused

TIE is a coach for self-improvement, not a tool for judgment. We help teachers move beyond test scores toward meaningful growth.

How TIE Works

Multimodal AI analyzing four key teaching dimensions

1. Visual Analysis

MediaPipe analyzes facial expressions and body language to understand engagement and delivery.

Tracks 478 facial landmarks

2. Content Analysis

Whisper transcribes speech, then Gemini AI analyzes clarity, jargon, fillers, and structure.

Teaching clarity score

3. Vocal Analysis

LibROSA analyzes voice modification including tone, pace, energy, and pitch variance.

Modulation & pacing insights

4. Interaction Analysis

Pyannote.audio performs speaker diarization to track student talk-time ratio.

Classroom interaction balance
